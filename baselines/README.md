# Baselines

The manuscript compares HySure, CSTF, PSRT, MH-FUNet, SDP, MCIFNet, SFIGNet, EMR-Diff and S3RNet. Their implementations, weights and datasets are not redistributed in this repository. Obtain each method from its original authors and follow its applicable license.

The public utilities here do not reproduce the baseline training schedules or model adaptations. A fair comparison also requires the registered data splits, source versions, scoring masks, observation operators and checkpoints, none of which are supplied here.
